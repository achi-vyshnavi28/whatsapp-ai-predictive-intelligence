## Summary
- **50.8%** of accounts are churned under a 90-day-silence definition -- a concrete, actionable target list, not a vague 'engagement' metric.
- A logistic regression on nothing but RFM + tenure reaches **71.5% accuracy / 0.780 ROC-AUC** -- strong enough to rank accounts by churn risk for proactive outreach.
- The Cox model quantifies *why*: order frequency is the strongest protective factor, meaning a conversational-commerce nudge that gets one more order out of a slowing account measurably extends its lifetime, not just its optics.
- The Kaplan-Meier curve gives a concrete median-lifetime number to set SLAs and outreach timing against, instead of guessing when to intervene.
- The composite Health Score turns 3 separate metrics (churn probability, recency, RFM tier) into **1 sortable worklist number**: 1,883 accounts (32.0%) fall below the at-risk threshold today.
